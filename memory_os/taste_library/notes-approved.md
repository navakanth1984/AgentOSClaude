## What "Excellent" Means in This Domain

Based on CLAUDE.md capture format and your preferences:

### Required Frontmatter
```yaml
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [tag1, tag2]
project: "Project Name"
source: "URL or description"
---
```

### Required Sections
- **Key Idea** — the one insight worth keeping; could stand alone
- **Details** — supporting context, not a full transcript
- **Action / Next Steps** — at least one checkbox (`- [ ] ...`)

### Style Rules
- **File naming**: `YYYY-MM-DD-kebab-case-title.md` — always
- **Inbox first**: new captures always land in `00-Inbox/` unless explicitly told otherwise
- **Tags**: at minimum 2 tags — one for project, one for type (e.g., `ai-tools`, `learning`, `cinematic`)
- **Source always cited**: URL, "Personal experiment", "Claude session", or "YouTube: [title]"
- **Concise**: if Key Idea is more than 3 sentences, it's not distilled enough
- **Link to project**: `project: "Agent OS"` or `project: "DAAVA"` — always fill this field
