## What "Excellent" Means in This Domain

Based on your established preferences:

- **Dialogue**: Subtext over text — characters never say what they mean directly
- **Scene length**: Tight. A scene that could be 1 page should never be 2
- **Beat discipline**: Every scene has a clear turning point; if there's no turn, cut the scene
- **Action lines**: Sensory and specific — camera-aware without being a shot list
- **Character voice**: Each character sounds like only that character; no voice bleed
- **Telugu/Indian context (DAAVA)**: Cultural specificity earns authenticity; generics don't
