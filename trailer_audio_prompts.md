## 🎙️ PART 1: ElevenLabs Voiceover & Character Dubbing Script

To maintain physical authenticity (adhering to the `/direction-skill` Biryani and exhaustion methods), instruct the voice actors as specified in the cues.

### Character Profiles (ElevenLabs Voice Design)
* **The Tail (Vaalagaathram - Narrator):** 
  * *Voice Spec:* Deep, warm, dry, slightly raspy male voice. Nostalgic but ancient, conveying centuries of quiet observation. Like a dry wind sweeping across stone.
  * *Settings:* High stability (75%), mid clarity (65%), style exaggeration (10%).
* **Lord Ravana:**
  * *Voice Spec:* Booming, chest-heavy, echoing male baritone. Exudes absolute tyranny and cruel amusement. Deep vocal register with high resonance.
  * *Settings:* Low stability (40% to allow emotional shifts), high clarity (80%).
* **Lord Rama:**
  * *Voice Spec:* Calm, clear, resonant, and balanced male tenor. Completely steady, no crackle or rasp, conveying divine focus.

-### Movement-by-Movement Voice Script & Performance Cues

## 🎵 PART 2: Google Flow Music (Lyria 3 Pro) Scoring Prompts

Each prompt is built using the **8-Layer Music Prompt Anatomy** to ensure a high-fidelity cinematic blend of traditional South Indian instrumentation and modern trailer scale.

### Prompt 1: Movement 1 (The Overlooked Wind)
```text
[GENRE] Cinematic South Indian mythological drone, ancient ambient orchestration.
[MOOD] Ominous, minimalist, undercurrent of deep suspense.
[INSTRUMENTATION] Low sustained drone (Kharaharapriya raga, Sa note on electronic tambura), distant bansuri flute with heavy reverb, slow, dry sweeping wood percussion (simulating carpet sweeping).
[TEMPO] Rubato (free time), slow breath-like pacing, 50 BPM.
[STRUCTURE] [Intro] Drone only, bansuri enters at bar 4 with a single breathy note. [Build] Low string ostinato (cellos) starts at bar 8.
[NEGATIVE PROMPT] No drums, no vocals, no modern synthesizer, no major key progressions.
```
