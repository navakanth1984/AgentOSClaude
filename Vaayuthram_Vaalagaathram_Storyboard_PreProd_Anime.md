## 👥 CHARACTER SHEETS (Nano Banana Pro)
Use these prompts to establish consistent characters. Copy and paste each prompt block exactly as written.

### 1. HANUMAN - GIANT FORM
```text
Character reference sheet, cinematic anime style. Four angles of the same character in a single image: front-facing, 3/4 left, profile, rear view. Hanuman in giant warrior form. Colossal powerful vanara physiology, muscular frame, clean-lined golden-tan fur texture, expressive golden-colored eyes, sharp jawline. Wearing simple cream-white cotton waist wraps, a dark bronze chest band across his chest. Neutral grey studio background, even diffused light, no shadows, no weapons.
```

```text
Character reference sheet, cinematic anime style. Four angles of the same character in a single image: front-facing, 3/4 left, profile, rear view. Hanuman in small house-cat-sized form. ### 3. THE TAIL (THREE STATES)
```
```text
Object reference sheet, cinematic anime style. Three panels in a single horizontal strip representing three states of Hanuman's tail. Panel 1: "AT REST" coiled tail on red sand, realistic golden-tan fur line art, muscular root tapering to a tip, low side light. Panel 2: "BANNER" streaming straight horizontally with motion blur at the tip, looking like wind-blown fabric. Panel 3: "BURNING PEN" tail engulfed in orange-white anime flames, tip glowing like white fire ink on black background.
```
