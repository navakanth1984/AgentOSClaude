# Vaayuthram Vaalagaathram
> A creative trailer project retelling the Sundarakanda of the Ramayana from the unique POV of Hanuman's sentient tail, Vaalagaathram.

## Overview
[Vaayuthram Vaalagaathram](../Vaayuthram_Vaalagaathram_Trailer.md) is an animated teaser trailer project targeted at kids and teenagers. The story shifts the focus of Valmiki's Sundarakanda to Hanuman's sentient tail, exploring themes of devotion, loyalty, and the transformation of a tool meant for humiliation into a weapon of liberation.

## Characters & Production Profile
* **Hanuman & Vaalagaathram (The Tail):** Retold as a buddy-team dynamic. The tail has its own personality, a silver ring piercing with a bell, and expresses emotions by clinching (anger) or fluffing (happiness).
* **Art Style:** Blends classic 1990s Chandamama comic book art by Sankar and Vankar with modern webtoon action-line aesthetics to capture teenage interest.
* **Production Plan:** Adheres to the approved [Audio-First Animation Workflow](../memory_os/taste_library/audio_first_animation_workflow.md) to preserve video generation credits by locking the ElevenLabs voice track and Flow Music score before rendering.
